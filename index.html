What week is it today?

Weekify shows the current ISO week number for today's date. ISO weeks begin on Monday, and week 1 is the week containing the year's first Thursday. The large number above updates automatically from your device date.

What day of the year is it?

The second card shows today's ordinal day: a number from 1 to 365, or 366 in a leap year. Tap either card to browse complete week or day archives for other years.

How to use Weekify

  1. Read the current week number or day-of-year number on the main screen.
  2. Tap a card to open the full archive for the current year.
  3. Use Previous and Next to compare dates in other years, then use Today to return to the current date.
